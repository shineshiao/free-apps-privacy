Privacy Policy

Shift Calendar · Effective 15 October 2026 · Last updated 15 October 2026

This policy explains what the Shift Calendar mobile application (“the app”) collects, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the Android version of the app published under the package name com.shineshiao.shiftcalendar.

Shift Calendar shows your rotating work schedule: you set your shift pattern once and see every day’s shift, with a home-screen widget. It has no accounts and no login, and your schedule never leaves your device.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. You never give the app your name, email address, employer, or any other contact detail.
  • Your shift pattern and the days you change stay on your device. They are not uploaded to us, and we cannot see them.
  • The app shows ads and uses analytics and crash reporting. These use industry-standard identifiers, described below, and never include your schedule.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Information you give us

None. The app has no sign-up, no sign-in, no profile, and no contact form. We do not ask you for personal information and we have no way to receive it through the app.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, on your phone or tablet only:

  • Your shift rotation: the cycle of shifts (for example “2 days, 2 nights, 4 off”) and the date it starts from.
  • The individual days you change, such as a swapped shift, overtime or leave.
  • The app language you choose, if you pick one in Settings.
  • A copy of your rotation and the words the home-screen widget shows, kept in the app’s private storage so the widget can work out today’s and tomorrow’s shift on its own.

This data never leaves your device through anything we operate. We have no server that receives it and no ability to read it. Deleting the app deletes all of it permanently, and because it is stored only on your device we cannot recover or restore it for you.

Device backups. If you have Android’s own backup turned on, Android may include the app’s data in your device backup to your Google account. That is handled by Google under your device settings and your Google account; we do not receive it. You can turn it off in your device’s backup settings.

The home-screen widget displays today’s and tomorrow’s shift and your next day off on your home screen. Anyone who can see your home screen can see them, so only add the widget if you are comfortable with that.

Permissions

The app does not ask for any permissions while you use it. It does not request access to your notifications, camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, photos or files. Like all apps that show ads and send crash reports, it uses internet access, which Android grants without asking.

Information collected automatically

The app uses Google services that collect limited technical and usage data. None of it includes your shift pattern or schedule:

ServiceWhat it collectsWhy
Google AdMob Your device's advertising identifier, approximate (non-precise) location inferred from IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data To select, deliver, and measure the ads that keep the app free
Firebase Analytics A randomly generated app instance ID, device model, operating system version, country, and basic usage events such as app opens To understand how the app is used, so it can be improved
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reports: stack traces, device state, operating system version, and a randomly generated installation ID To diagnose and fix crashes
Firebase Remote Config A device installation ID and basic device attributes, sent when fetching settings To adjust app settings (such as ad settings) without shipping an update

None of this data identifies you by name. We see it only in aggregate, through Google's dashboards.

Ratings. After you first set up your rotation, the app may ask Google Play to show its standard in-app rating prompt, once. The prompt and any rating you give are handled by Google Play; we do not see who rated.

Advertising

The app is free and is funded by advertising. It displays a banner ad and occasional full-screen ads after a finished task, never when the app opens. Ads are supplied by Google AdMob.

AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show ads relevant to your interests and to limit how often you see the same ad. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices: On Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation. Opting out does not remove ads from the app — it makes them less relevant to you.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing. Data is processed only by the service providers listed above (Google LLC and its affiliates), acting on our behalf. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of users.

International transfers

The Google services above process data on servers that may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Google maintains its own safeguards for these transfers, described in its privacy policy.

Retention

Data stored on your device stays until you change it in the app or uninstall the app. Analytics and crash data are retained by Google under its standard retention periods, generally no longer than 14 months for analytics event data.

Children

The app is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Security

Data kept on your device is stored in the app’s private storage, protected by the operating system. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we rely on Google's encrypted connections for all data the app sends.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal information — including under the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation, the California Consumer Privacy Act, Brazil’s LGPD, Turkey’s KVKK, Indonesia’s PDP Law and India’s DPDP Act.

Because the app holds no account and no personal profile, most of this data is under your direct control:

  • To delete your rotation, your changed days and other data the app has stored, uninstall the app (or clear its storage in Android’s app settings).
  • To reset the identifier used for ads, use the device settings listed under Advertising above.
  • For any other request, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes (for example, if new features change what is collected). The effective date at the top will be revised, and material changes will be noted in the app's store listing. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
